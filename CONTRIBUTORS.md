## Credit policy

Anyone who tests the Atlas and sends feedback is a contributor. You choose how you're named (full name, initials or handle) and can be removed any time by e-mailing hello@kaitiwu.de. Contributors are listed alphabetically; there is no ranking.

**Roles:** tester · feedback · place (added or confirmed a place) · data · translation · design · code

## How a name gets here (demo phase)

1. Someone sends the feedback form on kaitiwu.de and ticks "Yes, list me as a contributor".
2. Add them to `contributors` in `config.js` and to this file.
3. When their suggestion ships, add a `changelog` entry in `config.js` with their name under `thanks`.
